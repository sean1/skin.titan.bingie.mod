## Historical Formula 1 classifications

`modules/f1_archive_sessions.json` is adapted from data by the F1DB contributors in [F1DB v2026.20.0](https://github.com/f1db/f1db/releases/tag/v2026.20.0) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The inventory keeps positive timing or lap evidence from individual session classifications and maps race identities to BINGIE’s existing calendar. It omits aggregate qualifying when the format had separate sessions. Historical reports corroborate separate pre-qualifying sessions and supply dates where available, including the [1965 South African Grand Prix](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/february-1965/28/11th-south-african/).

Contemporary practice reports confirm two Saturday track sessions at all 16 races in 2003 and supply local dates. The FIA [classified the two sessions together through round eight](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/2003/jun/report.php/id/10913/.html).

Contemporary race reports supply dates for 17 race-day warm-ups between 1972 and 1983, including the [1972 United States Grand Prix](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/november-1972/28/united-states-grand-prix/) and the [1983 San Marino Grand Prix](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1983/33/san-marino-grand-prix-8/). Formula 1's [qualifying format history](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/deciding-the-grid-a-history-of-f1-qualifying-formats.1oh1hemlnZ4x9rt2nff8vn) confirms the move to one Saturday qualifying session after the 2005 Monaco Grand Prix.

Contemporary reports distinguish the qualifying practice days at Monaco in [1961](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1961/11/xix-grand-prix-of-monaco-what-a-race/), [1963 and 1964](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1964/14/reflections-on-the-monaco-gp/), [1970](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1970/25/the-28th-monaco-grand-prix/), [1971](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-1971/44/the-29th-monaco-grand-prix/) and [1975](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1975/26/33rd-monaco-grand-prix/).

Contemporary reports also supply qualifying practice sessions for Britain in [1950](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1950/7/the-royal-silverstone-meeting/), [1956](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/august-1956/16/the-british-grand-prix-11/) and [1964](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/august-1964/58/xvii-british-grand-prix/), [Belgium 1956](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-1956/13/grand-prix-de-belgique/), [Italy 1959](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/october-1959/15/the-xxxth-italian-grand-prix/) and the Dutch Grands Prix in [1962](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1962/12/the-dutch-grand-prix-11/), [1963](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/august-1963/20/the-dutch-grand-prix-2/), [1964](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-1964/23/the-dutch-grand-prix-12/) and [1965](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/august-1965/13/dutch-grand-prix-4/).

Qualifying in [Japan 2010](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/eng/result_s/2010/f1/1010_f1_q.html) and [Austin 2015](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/qualifying-postponed-until-sunday-morning-in-storm-swept-austin.5jSYeIam5qQqDUrtIaFKiV) moved to Sunday. [Force India's report](https://au.motorsport.com/f1/news/japanese-gp-force-india-saturday-report/2509968/) confirms the 10:00 local start at Suzuka.

At the [2013 Australian Grand Prix](https://press.pirelli.com/2013-australian-grand-prix---qualifying/), Q1 took place on Saturday and rain pushed Q2 and Q3 to Sunday morning.

For Japan 2004, the [FIA's revised timetable](https://www.racecar.com/news/8175/motorsport/qualifying-postponed-due-to-typhoon) and [Toyota's race report](https://toyotagazooracing.com/archive/ms/en/F1archive/season/gp2004/17_japan/04_jpn_05.html) distinguish the two Sunday qualifying sessions and the Friday practices.

Contemporary [Associated Press](https://dailyiowan.lib.uiowa.edu/DI/1955/di1955-05-21.pdf) and [United Press](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn97071090/1955-05-23/ed-1/seq-9/ocr/) reports establish the four 1955 Indianapolis 500 time-trial days. The [Speedway's four-lap records](https://www.indianapolismotorspeedway.com/events/indy500/history/historical-stats/race-stats/records/four-lap-qualification-records) corroborate the May 15 runs.

The Indianapolis Motor Speedway's four-lap records and the contemporary [May 21 race coverage](https://manchesterhistory.org/News/Manchester%20Evening%20Hearld_1956-05-21.pdf) confirm May 19 and 20 for the 1956 Indianapolis 500. An [Associated Press report](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn96088460/1956-05-29/ed-1/seq-7.pdf) confirms the shortened May 27 session and the rain cancellations for May 26 and the proposed May 28 makeup.

For the 1957 Indianapolis 500, [United Press](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn90051770/1957-05-20/ed-1/seq-7/ocr/) reported that Pole Day ran on May 18 and rain canceled May 19. The [Associated Press](https://gateway.okhistory.org/ark:/67531/metadc2112012/m1/7/) covered May 25 and the final session on May 26, when nine cars were bumped.

For the 1959 Indianapolis 500, the [Speedway's qualifying records](https://www.indianapolismotorspeedway.com/events/indy500/history/historical-stats/race-stats/records/one-lap-qualification-records) confirm May 16. Contemporary coverage confirms [May 17](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn99063813/1959-05-18/ed-1/seq-11.pdf), [May 23](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn97071090/1959-05-24/ed-1/seq-9.pdf) and [May 24](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn97071090/1959-05-25/ed-1/seq-8/ocr/), when two cars were bumped by faster qualifiers.

Contemporary [Autosport](https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/lib/magazines/autosport/1960/AS1960.06.17.pdf) and [United Press International](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn97071090/1960-05-23/ed-1/seq-9.pdf) reports establish the four 1960 Indianapolis 500 time-trial days. Drivers started qualifying attempts between showers on May 21, but none completed a run that day.

Toyota's contemporary team timetables and session reports supply practice and qualifying dates for [2006](https://toyotagazooracing.com/archive/ms/public/en/gp/), [2007](https://toyotagazooracing.com/archive/ms/public/en/gp2007/), [2008](https://toyotagazooracing.com/archive/ms/public/en/gp2008/), [Australia 2009](https://toyotagazooracing.com/archive/ms/en/F1archive/season/gp2009/01_australia/index.html) and [Malaysia 2009](https://toyotagazooracing.com/archive/ms/en/F1archive/season/gp2009/02_malaysia/index.html).

Toyota's contemporary reports supply session dates for all races in [2002](https://toyotagazooracing.com/archive/ms/en/F1archive/season/gp2002/) and [2004](https://toyotagazooracing.com/archive/ms/en/F1archive/season/gp2004/). Contemporary Atlas F1 practice reports distinguish individual practice periods, including Australia in [2002](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/2002/mar/report.php/id/6552/.html) and [2004](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/2004/mar/report.php/id/12667/.html).

Contemporary Atlas F1 reports supply local session dates for the [2001 season](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/2001/mar/), including the [cancellation of the first Saturday practice in Belgium](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/2001/sep/report.php/id/5412/.html).

Contemporary [Atlas F1 reports](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/2000/mar/) and [NewsOnF1 timing tables](https://www.newsonf1.com/2000/sched.htm) establish the dates and distinguish the four physical practice sessions at each race in 2000.

Contemporary [Atlas F1 reports](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/1999/may1999.html) supply local session dates for 1999. The [FIA weekend timetable](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/1999fiafaq.htm), contemporary reports such as [British Saturday practice](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/second-practice-second-session-latest-5050241/5050241/), and F1DB lap counts corroborate the two Saturday practice periods.

Contemporary [Atlas F1 reports](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/1998/657.htm) and the [Verstappen team news archive](https://news.verstappen.com/en/archive/1998/5) supply local session dates for 1998. The [FIA timetable](https://www.jomenvisst.de/fia/1998SportingRegulations/1998_FIA_Formula_One_World_Championship_Sporting_Regulations.htm), Formula 1 timing, and separate timing reports such as [Silverstone's first Saturday practice](https://www.zonef1.com/saisons/1998/courses/grande-bretagne/libres-21.php) and [Suzuka's first Saturday practice](https://www.zonef1.com/saisons/1998/courses/japon/libres-21.php) corroborate distinct Saturday runs. Brazil retains its daily practice classifications.

The 2026 replay pack manifests use identifiers from public release notes, including [Monaco](https://www.reddit.com/r/MotorsportsReplays/comments/1tzbjjw/comment/oqbu68y/) and [São Paulo](https://www.reddit.com/r/MotorsportsReplays/comments/1xoqc7s/formula_1_2026_r20_sao_paulo_grand_prix_sky/). TorBox supplied the file lists for the exact identifiers.
