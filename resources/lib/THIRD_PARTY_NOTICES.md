## Historical Formula 1 classifications

`modules/f1_archive_sessions.json` is adapted from data by the F1DB contributors in [F1DB v2026.20.0](https://github.com/f1db/f1db/releases/tag/v2026.20.0) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The inventory keeps positive timing or lap evidence from individual session classifications and maps race identities to BINGIE’s existing calendar. It omits aggregate qualifying when the format had separate sessions. Historical reports corroborate separate pre-qualifying sessions and supply dates where available, including the [1965 South African Grand Prix](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/february-1965/28/11th-south-african/).

Contemporary practice reports confirm two Saturday track sessions at all 16 races in 2003 and supply local dates. The FIA [classified the two sessions together through round eight](https://www.atlasf1.com/news/2003/jun/report.php/id/10913/.html).

Contemporary race reports supply dates for 17 race-day warm-ups between 1972 and 1983, including the [1972 United States Grand Prix](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/november-1972/28/united-states-grand-prix/) and the [1983 San Marino Grand Prix](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1983/33/san-marino-grand-prix-8/). Formula 1's [qualifying format history](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/deciding-the-grid-a-history-of-f1-qualifying-formats.1oh1hemlnZ4x9rt2nff8vn) confirms the move to one Saturday qualifying session after the 2005 Monaco Grand Prix.

Contemporary reports distinguish the qualifying practice days at Monaco in [1961](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1961/11/xix-grand-prix-of-monaco-what-a-race/), [1963 and 1964](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1964/14/reflections-on-the-monaco-gp/), [1970](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1970/25/the-28th-monaco-grand-prix/), [1971](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-1971/44/the-29th-monaco-grand-prix/) and [1975](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/june-1975/26/33rd-monaco-grand-prix/).
